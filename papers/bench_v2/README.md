# Bench v2 — auditing the denominator

Working title: **Auditing the Denominator: Coverage Census and Fault-Tested
Durability for Medical Vision–Language Model Benchmarks**.

Status: **v2, not yet submitted.** 16 pages, 3 figures, 11 tables, 31
references, compiled 2026-11-14 with `pdflatex` + `bibtex`, **zero undefined
citations, zero overfull boxes, zero LaTeX errors**.

Author block: Koshun Huang, School of Medical Imaging, North Sichuan Medical
College, Nanchong, Sichuan, China; 2720356281@qq.com; ORCID 0009-0006-1226-8145.
A double-blind venue takes `Anonymous Authors`.

What changed from v1: the paper no longer leads with "durable" (the weakest of
its claims) but with the denominator. Three pieces of new evidence were added
and all of it is measured on CPU, not asserted:

- **Baseline comparison** (`scripts/123_harness_experiments.py`, 2,000 cells):
  naive append, append+fsync, atomic whole-file rewrite, SQLite/WAL, and the
  shipped writer. The shipped writer costs 1.95 ms/cell and adds no per-row
  storage; SQLite adds 37%.
- **Fault injection** (nine faults at the syscall boundary): a crash loses 10/700
  rows under naive append and 0 under the shipped writer; two concurrent writers
  produce 500 and 699 duplicate keys under append/fsync and are refused by the
  file-replacing writers.
- **Denominator census** (`scripts/124_artifact_census.py`): recomputes each
  file's denominator from the file. Applied to 259 prediction files it reports
  255 uniform denominators, four non-uniform, and one duplicate key.

"Zero error rows" was retired and replaced by five invariants that can fail.

## Positioning

This is the engineering companion to `papers/main_v5/` (the measurement-validity
paper). The two papers share the artifact set but make different claims:

| Paper | Claim | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| `main_v5` | Four measurement preconditions reverse headline conclusions. | 275,218-call three-layer matrix; audits. |
| `bench_v1` | The harness makes those numbers reconstructible on CPU. | 38 stage entries, 39 files, 40/40 crash/resume checks, coverage census, locks. |

The companion paper reports the science; this paper reports the machinery.
Neither paper uses the other's experiments as independent evidence.
