\begin{table}[H]

\begin{tabular}{llrr}
\toprule
Pass label & Budget (tokens) & Archive rows & Distinct cells \\
\midrule

\midrule

\end{table}

The distinction matters for reporting: ``1,141 cells never terminate at 5,120'' is a cell-level statement, while ``4,283 archived failures'' counts attempts across four budgets. A reader who equates the two would over-state the residual by 3.8$\times$.
